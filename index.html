Chrono Saga

A brand-new time-hopping adventure. Shatter the Rift across 5 ages of history — 20 levels, all-new foes, and a Guardian at the end of every age.

Controls

⬅➡ or A/D — Move
⬆ / Space / W — Jump (hold longer = jump higher, double-tap for air-dash once unlocked)
Shift — Sprint
⬇ / S — Crouch
E / Tap ability button — Use your currently equipped Chrono ability
Stomp enemies from above to defeat them. Collect Time Shards and power-ups scattered through each age.

Power-ups:
⚡ Overcharge — bigger, stronger, breaks blocks
🔥 Ember Core — throw fireballs
🌟 Chrono Star — brief invincibility
🪽 Sky Wisp — slow-fall glide
🛡️ Aegis — absorbs one hit
🧲 Lodestone — pulls shards toward you
💨 Surge Boots — huge speed + air-dash
❄️ Stasis Field — freezes nearby enemies

On mobile: use the on-screen buttons.
